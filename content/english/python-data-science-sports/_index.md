## Introduction

Welcome to the Nuevo Sports League! Meet **Coach Luna**, the organizer of the league's big championship game. Six teams from six different cities are competing, and Coach Luna has one big question:

**Which city should host the championship?**

To answer it, she needs to look at **data**. **Data** is information we collect, like how many people live in a city or how many seats a stadium has. **Data science** is the job of using data to answer questions and make smart decisions. Data scientists do this for sports teams, hospitals, cities, and video games every day!

In this workshop you will become Coach Luna's data scientist. You will use Python to store population data, calculate totals and averages, sort cities into groups, draw charts, and finally pick the championship host.

![A cartoon whistle, a bar chart, and a trophy side by side, representing sports and data](media/data-sports-banner.svg)

## What you will learn

By the end of this workshop you will be able to:

* Store a table of data in Python lists
* Use loops to add up and average numbers
* Use `if` statements to sort data into groups
* Draw a simple chart from data
* Apply these skills to population data to answer a real question

## Prerequisites
