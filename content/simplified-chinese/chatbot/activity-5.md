## 回显通配符  
让我们一起使用通配符 ```*``` 和 ```<star />``` 标签，让我们的机器人在回复中包含用户的输入内容。  
<img src="../img/learn.png" alt="渴望学习新概念的卡通形象插图" width="20%" style="display: block; margin-left: 0;" />  

1. 如果用户输入 "My name is [name]"（我的名字是 [名字]），机器人应该回复 "Nice to meet you, [name]!"（很高兴认识你，[名字]！）。  
示例：  
输入 | 输出  
---|--------------  
My name is John | Nice to meet you, John!  
My name is Jane | Nice to meet you, Jane!  
My name is Bob | Nice to meet you, Bob!  

2. 如果用户输入 "My favorite color is [color]"（我最喜欢的颜色是 [颜色]），机器人应该回复 "I also like [color]!"（我也喜欢 [颜色]！）。  
示例：  
输入 | 输出  
---|--------------  
My favorite color is blue | I also like blue!  
My favorite color is red | I also like red!  
My favorite color is green | I also like green!  

3. 如果用户输入 "My favorite food is [food]"（我最喜欢的食物是 [食物]），机器人应该回复 "[food]! Yum!"（[食物]！真美味！）。  
示例：  
输入 | 输出  
---|--------------  
My favorite food is pizza | pizza! Yum!  
My favorite food is pasta | pasta! Yum!  
My favorite food is sushi | sushi! Yum!
